Let’s get your Turkey eSIM running

Thanks for your order. If this is your first eSIM, you need to install it and switch it on. It is easier than it sounds. Follow the steps for your phone below; your QR code and activation details are at the end of this email.
Before you start. Stay on Wi-Fi through the whole install. An eSIM installs once, on one device. A failed or repeated attempt cannot be undone.
You can install anywhere: at home before you fly, or after you land. Installing does not start your plan.
 
1 Installation
Please note: an eSIM can be installed only once, and only on one device. Keep the phone on Wi-Fi until the plan finishes downloading.
There are three ways to add your nowsim eSIM. Pick whichever suits the phone in your hand.
Fast installation  iPhone, iOS 17.4 and later
1. Open this email on the iPhone that will use the eSIM.
2. Tap Install eSIM in one tap in the second card at the end of this email.
3. Follow the prompts iOS shows you. Do not interrupt the process while the plan downloads.
Installation via QR code  iPhone, iPad, and Android
You need a second screen for this: open the QR code at the end of this email on a laptop or another phone, then scan it with the phone that will use the eSIM.
1. Open the Add eSIM screen iPhone and iPad: Settings → Mobile Data → Add eSIM Android: Settings → Network & internet → SIMs → Add eSIM
2. Scan the QR code from this email Point the camera at the code on the other screen and follow the on-screen instructions.
3. On iPhone, select ‘Abroad’, then ‘Data Only’ The plan is for travel outside your country, and it carries internet only. Calls and SMS are not included.
4. Let the plan download Stay on Wi-Fi until the download finishes.
5. Label the new plan ‘nowsim’ It makes the line easy to find later. You can rename it in Settings at any time.
Manual installation  when the QR code cannot be scanned
Open the same Add eSIM screen and type the details in by hand. They are in the third card at the end of this email. iPhone and iPad: tap Enter Details Manually, paste the SM-DP+ address and the activation code, and leave the confirmation code empty. Android: tap Need help?, then Enter it manually, and paste the activation code.
More information Step-by-step guides with screenshots: iOS devices and Android devices. Not sure your phone takes an eSIM? Check the compatible devices list, or read the help centre.
 
2 Activation
Once the eSIM is installed, three settings switch it on. Do these when you land, or before you fly — either works.
1. Turn the nowsim line on iPhone and iPad: Settings → Mobile Data → nowsim → Turn On This Line Android: Settings → Network & internet → SIMs → nowsim After enabling, go back and reopen the screen to refresh the plan.
2. Turn on Data Roaming On the nowsim line only, in the same screen. nowsim runs on partner networks, so the line stays offline without roaming.
3. Send mobile data over nowsim Pick nowsim as the SIM used for mobile data. On iPhone, switch off Allow Mobile Data Switching so your home number is never charged.
Once roaming is on, it can take 5 to 10 minutes to find a network the first time.
